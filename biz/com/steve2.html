§ II

A Letter, Half-Mad

† ‡ §

left under the door of the launderette. fountain pen, brown ink, no envelope.

I do not call this a brand. I call it a concern. the difference is that a brand wants you, and a concern is something you have whether you like it or not.

there is no marketing here. there is a still. her name is Margaret. she hums in F. she has hummed in F since 1974. she will outlive me. she will outlive you. she may already have done so.

every bottle is decanted by hand. some are decanted at three in the morning. some are decanted on wednesday sunday. some never. these are the most expensive ones.

i do not believe in top notes, heart notes, base notes. i believe in the smell when you walk in, the smell when you forget you walked in, and the smell that follows you home and stays in the coat.

if you cannot afford a bottle, write to me. if you can afford ten bottles, write to me less often.

the green jar is not for sale. please stop asking. it is listed only so the lot register stays consecutive.

i do not ship on fridays. i do not answer the door on sundays. i do not pick up the phone if it rings twice. these are not eccentricities. they are load-bearing.

if a perfume works for you, you will know within four hours and never again. if it works for you for a long time, that is something else, and i cannot help with it.

that is the whole letter. i was going to write more but the dog came back.

— Stevesigned in copper, 4:11 a.m., a tuesday

§ V

Margaret

♀ ⚯ ♀

the still. the matriarch. the reason any of this exists.

Margaretcopper alembic, 18 litres, in continuous use since 1974

she came from a closed pharmacy in Brittany, was wrapped in a wool blanket, and was carried up two flights of stairs by four men, none of whom would later return for the rest of the agreed payment.

she hums in F. it is not a metaphor. with the door closed and the burner on, she produces a steady tone at approximately 87 hertz. steve has tuned a guitar to her on at least one occasion.

she is wiped down every tuesday. she is not used on fridays. she has been repaired twice — once for a cracked worm in 1998, once for a reason steve will not specify in 2017.

every bottle in the rayons, every soap on the shelf, and the green jar — all of them — passed through her at some point.

est. 1974
capacity 18 L
hum F₂ · 87.3 Hz

§ VIII

Errata

⌯ ⌯ ⌯

corrections to claims that were not, technically, wrong.

amended · iv.x

The Following Have Been Corrected

  1. § I, line 3. Margaret was acquired in 1974, not the year of the long winter, although both are technically correct and one is more useful.
  2. § II.viii. "four hours" was previously written as "four days." this was a typographical error introduced by the typewriter, which Steve no longer trusts.
  3. § IV, Lot XXVII. the green jar is not, as previously implied, warm. it is sometimes warm. these are not the same.
  4. § Hours. "do not come on sunday" was, for a period of three weeks in 2019, "come only on sunday". this was not a correction. this was a different policy. it has now ended.
  5. § Avis, M. of Toulouse. the bus station was, in fact, a tram stop. the stranger was, in fact, the same person submitting the review. the recovery was, by their own later admission, partial.
  6. § Lot XLI. earlier copy referred to Lot XLI as "imaginary." this was inaccurate. Lot XLI does not exist, which is a stronger claim and harder to refute.
